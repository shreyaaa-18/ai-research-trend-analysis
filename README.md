<b> AI Research Trend Analysis: </b> </br>
A machine learning project that applies unsupervised learning techniques to discover thematic clusters and emerging trends within artificial intelligence research literature sourced from arXiv.</br>
</br>
<b> Overview: </b> </br>
This project builds an end-to-end NLP and clustering pipeline on a dataset of AI research paper titles and abstracts. The goal is to automatically identify latent research themes without relying on predefined labels, and to visualise how topics are distributed and interconnected across the corpus.</br>
